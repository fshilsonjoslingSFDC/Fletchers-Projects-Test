FAQ

Common Questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do you service areas outside of Griffith?
Yes — we cover Griffith and the surrounding Murrumbidgee district, including Yenda, Hanwood, Leeton, Narrandera, and Darlington Point. If you're unsure whether we can get to you, give us a ring and we'll let you know.
Are you licensed and insured for all plumbing and gas work?
Yes. All work is carried out by a fully licensed plumber and gasfitter, with public liability insurance in place. We can provide licence and insurance details on request — just ask.
Do you handle emergency callouts?
Yes. Call us directly on +61 410 479 267 — describe what's happening and we'll do our best to get to you as quickly as possible. Burst pipes, gas leaks, and sewage backups are always treated as urgent.
Will I get a quote before work starts?
Every time. We'll assess the job and give you a clear price before anything is touched. You won't be handed an invoice you weren't expecting.
What should I do if I suspect a gas leak?
Leave the building immediately. Don't switch any electrical switches on or off, and don't use a phone indoors. Once you're clear, call your gas supplier's emergency line. After that, call us to locate and fix the source properly. Don't return inside until the building has been cleared.
How do I know when a hot water system needs replacing?
Watch for slow heating, rust or discolouration in the hot water, a rumbling or popping noise from the unit, or visible corrosion on the tank. If your system is over 10–12 years old, it's worth getting it checked before it fails at a bad time.
Can you issue compliance certificates for gas work?
Yes. As a licensed gasfitter we can issue compliance certificates for gas installations and fit-outs where they're required — for new builds, renovations, or property sales.
How long will a typical job take?
It depends on the job. A tap replacement is usually under an hour. A full hot water system changeover is typically half a day. A bathroom fit-out takes longer. We'll give you a realistic timeframe when we quote.
